State of the Studio, 2025 Edition December 3, 2025

1. Wowsers, a year!

I started setting things up for the channel in December of last year. I didn’t actually start streaming until mid-January but December is where the idea started to become reality. So let’s talk about the state of Studios.

This might be a little confusing to people that aren’t me, so let me clarify real quick.

Mongoose Studios is a vehicle for my ideas/ventures/brand. Streaming is one thing that Mongoose Studios does. Software development is another thing that I do, even if a lot of it is on stream. If I were to branch out into anything else I would try to bring it under the Mongoose Studios brand in some way that made sense.
